mission "To Earth Day celebration [0]"
	name `地球日庆典`
	job
	repeat
	description `将 <fare> 在 <date> 之前运送至 <destination>，以参加4月22日的地球日庆祝活动。报酬为 <payment>。`
	passengers 4 4 .5
	deadline 0 2
	to offer
		or
			and
				random < 60
				month == 2
				day >= 6
			and
				random < 80
				month == 3
			and
				random < 20
				month == 4
				day < 22
		"days until year end" - 2 * "hyperjumps to system: Sol" > 253
		has "landing access: Earth"
	to fail
		month * 100 + day >= 422
	source
		near "Sol" 1 15
		government "Republic" "Free Worlds" "Syndicate" "Neutral"
	destination "Earth"
	on visit
		dialog phrase "generic passenger on visit"
	on complete
		payment 1000 180
		dialog "你祝愿你的 <passengers> 在 <planet> 上好运连连。在收取了 <payment> 的报酬后，你的 <passengers> 戴上地球日帽子，带着行李离开了。"
