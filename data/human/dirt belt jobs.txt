mission "Drought Relief"
	name `干旱救援：前往<planet>`
	job
	repeat
	description `农业世界<destination>目前正遭受干旱。请在<date>之前将<cargo>运送至该星球，可获得<payment>。`
	cargo "drought relief supplies" 25 2 .05
	deadline
	to offer
		random < 10
	source
		government "Republic"
		not attributes "farming"
	destination
		distance 3 10
		attributes "dirt belt"
		attributes "farming"
	on visit
		dialog phrase "generic cargo on visit"
	on complete
		payment 10000 160
		dialog "你交付了<commodity>货物，并领到了<payment>报酬。一群来自<planet>的市民向你致谢并与你道别。"


mission "To Remembrance Day celebration [0]"
	name `追忆日庆典`
	job
	repeat
	description `请在<date>之前将乘客<fare>送至<destination>，以参加2月19日的追忆日庆典。报酬为<payment>。`
	passengers 4 4 .5
	deadline
	to offer
		or
			and
				random < 20
				month == 12
				day >= 21
			and
				random < 30
				month == 1
			and
				random < 10
				month == 2
				day < 19
	to fail
		month * 100 + day >= 219
	source
		attributes "dirt belt"
	destination
		distance 3 15
		attributes "dirt belt"
	on visit
		dialog phrase "generic passenger on visit"
	on complete
		payment 1000 180
		dialog "你祝愿乘客<passengers>在<planet>一切顺利。脸上涂着绿色彩绘的孩子们跑下了飞船，他们的父母付给了你<payment>。"
